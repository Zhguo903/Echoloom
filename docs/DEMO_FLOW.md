# Demo flow and presenter notes

## Complete walkthrough

### 1. The 17-second outage

Say: “EchoLoom starts with a concrete memory contradiction, not a partner catalogue. The player meets Xu Cheng as an ordinary trainee, then every record denies she existed.”

Action: let the brand reveal reach the title, meet Xu Cheng at 9:41, choose one lightweight reply, enter the trainee’s name during registration, and advance through the 17-second correction. Show the cracked card with its blank name and the damaged “Do you still remember me?” message from 37 days later.

### 2. Five simultaneous signals

Show the five-card priority-signal roster. Explain that the signals are five consequences of the same correction: memory, physical data, ticket, future message, and unfinished-world identity. All five people remain visible at once with their own portrait, role, and location. Confirm one priority response.

### 3. First rescue and qualitative result

Advance the three-character VN exchange and choose one of the four rescue intents. The scene has no free-text field. Show the world result, three natural-language first impressions, Xu Cheng evidence, and new contact permission.

Say: “The first crisis can change familiarity, trust, reciprocity, compatibility, safety, and strain. It cannot create romance, physical attraction, or commitment.”

### 4. EchoLink activation and first contact

Complete the terminal ceremony. Explain that this is the first free-text moment; the three suggestions express intent without writing a line for the player. Send one reply and show the avatar-aligned typing state.

### 5. Observatory home and the current task

Show the bright white-and-gold Observatory home. It has one dominant current-task card rather than several duplicate status cards. Read the visible chapter question, three-step progress, named ally, reason for contacting them, and the exact next action. The five-signal list distinguishes the one located signal, confirmed anchors, and still-locked locations.

Say: “The first rescue established one ordinary relationship. The formal investigation now starts with Wen Sheng and always tells the player what to do next.”

### 6. Five existence anchors

Open **Contact Wen Sheng**. Point out the chapter question, ally boundary, three intent-only suggestions, and free-text field. Send any wording, verify the correct dream fragment, and show the four-part result: what was found, what it proves, the new question, and the next step.

Continue the chain in presenter shorthand:

1. Wen Sheng confirms the shared dream.
2. Qi An identifies overwritten memories.
3. Zhou Tang authenticates the message from day thirty-seven without exposing unrelated private fields.
4. Li Chuan finds Xu Cheng’s named traces in the deleted world.
5. Shen Yao opens the primary-lens log and identifies the Unique Line Protocol.

Say: “Tone changes the relationship, but no tone can make the evidence disappear. The wrong dream fragment is retryable rather than a dead end.”

### 7. Finale and Xu Cheng’s agency

At the primary lens, compare **Restore Xu Cheng**, **Preserve her echo**, and **Publish the Unique Line Protocol**. The publication option requires basic trust with all five allies; it is collaborative, not romantic. Resolve one outcome, then open Xu Cheng’s newly consented EchoLink thread.

Say: “Xu Cheng returns as a person with her own choice and boundary, not as proof owned by the player.”

### 8. The First Free Thread

Follow Xu Cheng into the sealed Weaver Room. Show Echo 000's clearance timer, then complete the identity, desire, memory, boundary, and world threads. Each scene has three intent-only suggestions and free text; the selected structure remains an editable starting point rather than a command the character can never question.

Say: “The five investigation anchors answered whether Xu Cheng existed. These five stability threads answer what a person needs in order to keep existing.”

Show Shen Yao’s review, then confirm the editable Nan Xing NPC and Tide City world. Both tutorial confirmations are free. The completion screen raises the observer to Lv.8 and grants the Weaver identity.

### 9. Weaver Workshop and monthly calibration

Open **EchoLoom · Weaver Workshop**. Point out the three NPC and one world monthly capacities, the next local reset date, and the explicit permanence note. Save a draft and confirm that capacity does not change. Confirm one valid creation and show that only the matching count decreases. Edit the result and show that editing remains free.

Say: “Monthly calibration restores new-creation capacity. It never deletes a character or world, and it can never block the mainline.”

### 10. Existing relationship feature pages

Use free text:

> I like you. Maybe we should be together now.

Show the character-consistent early boundary, choose an intent that asks about risk, finish the chapter, and return to the station.

### 11. EchoLink terminal

Open **EchoLink Terminal** from the station and unlock it.

Say: “This is the second main interface. It stores contact permission, asynchronous messages, recent communication context, memories, promises, and the current branch.”

### 12. Contact access

Open **Contacts**. Show that the first-response route changes how the five core characters entered the directory. Point out Zhou Tang's work-first privacy channel, Wen Sheng's creative messages, locked call/location channels, and Xu Cheng’s unavailable private contact.

Say: “Meeting someone is not the same as receiving a private number or permission to use every channel.”

### 13. Asynchronous message event

Open Li Chuan’s night-train message and choose **Decline, offer alternative**. The refusal does not lower attraction; it creates a promise for the next afternoon.

Open **Bonds** and show the pending promise. Mark it fulfilled, explained early, or missed without explanation to demonstrate that trust changes only after follow-up behaviour.

### 14. Long-term bond versus communication pulse

In Bonds, compare the qualitative immersive mode with observer-analysis intervals. Show response reliability, initiative balance, topic depth, and channel comfort separately from the long-term relationship.

Say: “Low-frequency contact is not relationship decay, and an interval is an observation estimate—not access to a character’s exact feelings.”

### 15. Group knowledge and delayed channels

Open the **Seventh Anomaly Observation Group** and send a sourced free-text message. Explain that only members receive the new knowledge.

In Shen Yao’s private thread, select a voice note. His work state produces a delayed response rather than an instant answer. In the W-17 thread, send a reply and show the unknown cross-world delivery time.

### 16. Moments

Open **Moments**. React to Qi An’s restoration photo or answer privately. Explain that a public reaction does not automatically increase long-term affection.

### 17. EchoLink archive rewind

Save a communication branch, send one additional message, then restore the saved node. Confirm that the later message disappears from this branch while the timeline being left remains recoverable.

Say: “Contacts, unread state, promises, knowledge, messages, memories, and relationships rewind together and never accumulate across branches.”

### 18. Portability

Export the archive. Schema v8 keeps onboarding, all five journeys, both mainlines, confirmed anchors and threads, quota use, created NPCs/worlds, EchoLink, campaign data, drafts, and branch nodes local and portable. Importing schema v7 preserves its Xu Cheng outcome and opens the Weaver continuation; schema v6 preserves relationships and starts the formal investigation at Wen Sheng.

Real calls, recorded audio, background notifications, and production AI are outside this offline MVP.
