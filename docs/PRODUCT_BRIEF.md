# EchoLoom Product Brief

## One-line positioning

A reversible, story-first multi-character relationship simulation in which a new trainee observer remembers Xu Cheng after a 17-second correction erases her from every record, then follows five kinds of evidence while developing independent relationships through shared mysteries, free conversation, conflict, boundaries, source-linked knowledge, and persistent memory.

## Core promise

The user is not selecting an assigned romantic partner. Qi An, Shen Yao, Li Chuan, Zhou Tang, and Wen Sheng each retain their own values, weaknesses, boundaries, knowledge, relationships, memories, and life outside the user. Friendship and distance are complete outcomes. Attraction, the player's wording, and a mutually confirmed relationship remain separate states.

## First prototype question

Will users understand that the first signal changes relationship context rather than assigning a partner; follow a five-step evidence chain without losing the current objective; and use visible, source-linked knowledge plus independent relationships to make a final decision they care about?

## Primary user for the first test

People who enjoy romance characters, interactive stories, or emotional AI interaction but do not want to write and maintain complex prompts.

## Secondary user

Advanced role-play, fan-fiction, dream-character, and co-creation users who want natural-language control, branch management, and memory transparency.

## Product principles

- Story creates attraction; chat extends the relationship.
- Stable core, learnable periphery.
- Memory is relationship infrastructure.
- Free text is primary; intent suggestions are optional.
- The character lives outside the chat.
- Conflict reveals values better than constant sweetness.
- Relationship change is explained in natural language, not only by a score.
- Character-to-player state, player expression, and mutual agreements are separate layers.
- A character can act only on information attributed to them.
- Rewind preserves the timeline being left and isolates branch state.
- Contact permission is part of the relationship: meeting someone does not automatically grant a private number or every channel.
- Recent communication context is separate from long-term relationship state; ordinary silence is not relationship decay.
- The user can choose interaction depth each time.
- No manipulative retention patterns.

## Demo scope

The demo includes a brand/title opening, Xu Cheng’s ordinary first meeting with the trainee, the interrupted identity registration and 17-second correction, her blank access card, the damaged message from 37 days later, a five-signal roster, five data-driven first-rescue routes, qualitative event results, an EchoLink activation ceremony, and the first free-text contact. A dynamic Observatory task card then leads through five implemented chapters in a fixed evidence order: Wen Sheng’s shared dream, Qi An’s overwritten memory, Zhou Tang’s future-message audit, Li Chuan’s deleted-world fragment, and Shen Yao’s primary-lens log. Each chapter retains free text, three intent-only suggestions, non-blocking evidence, a source-linked existence anchor, and one explicit next action. The finale offers restore, preserve-echo, and trust-gated publication outcomes, followed by Xu Cheng’s consent-based contact.

After the Xu Cheng finale, *The First Free Thread* turns creation into a story-earned responsibility: Echo 000 receives identity, desire, memory, boundary, and world threads from the existing cast; the player completes one free editable NPC/world tutorial and becomes an Lv.8 Weaver. The formal workshop provides three confirmed NPC creations and one confirmed world creation per local month, while drafts, edits, existing content, and mainline progress remain unrestricted. EchoLink, branch nodes, relationship/knowledge dossiers, typed memories, and character-continuity modes remain available.

Real calls, audio recording, background notifications, infinite procedural worlds, community sharing, explicit sexual scenes, and production model integration are deliberately outside this offline MVP.
