# 10–12 Minute Demo Script

## 1. Positioning and opening

“EchoLoom is a reversible, story-first multi-character relationship simulation. On the player’s first morning as a trainee, they meet Xu Cheng. A seventeen-second correction erases her from every record but fails to rewrite the player’s memory.”

Show the 9:41 meeting, temporary access card, trainee name entry, outage, blank card, failed search, and future message: “Do you still remember me?”

## 2. First response is not a partner choice

Show all five simultaneous signals and choose any rescue. Explain that this establishes the first ordinary collaboration, initial impressions, and contact permission. It never assigns a romantic partner and does not hide the other four people.

## 3. One legible current task

After the first contact, open the Observatory home. Read the one dominant task card: current question, three-step progress, next ally, reason, and exact action. Point out that the formal evidence chain starts with Wen Sheng even if another character was the first rescue.

## 4. Free response without a mainline dead end

Open Wen Sheng’s chapter. The three suggestions express intent rather than writing the player’s line, and the free-text input remains available. Choose any tone and investigate. Deliberately choose one wrong damaged-dream fragment, show the retry explanation, then choose the matching phrase.

Explain: “Relationship tone can change. Evidence cannot be lost because the player was cautious, direct, or emotionally distant.”

## 5. Five existence anchors

Show the result structure: what was found, what it proves, the new question, and the single next step. Summarise the playable chain:

- Wen Sheng — shared dream;
- Qi An — overwritten memory;
- Zhou Tang — future message;
- Li Chuan — deleted-world fragment;
- Shen Yao — primary-lens log.

Use Story to show confirmed, source-linked anchors and World to show the current investigation location.

## 6. Finale without a romance lock

At the primary lens, compare Restore Xu Cheng, Preserve her echo, and Publish the Unique Line Protocol. Publication requires basic trust with all five allies, not attraction. Choose one outcome and open Xu Cheng’s consent-based EchoLink contact.

“She returns as a person with her own boundary, not an unlockable reward or evidence owned by the player.”

## 7. EchoLink as the second main interface

Show Messages, Contacts, Moments, Bonds, and Archive. Contact sources and media permissions remain story-dependent. Ordinary silence is not punished; only explicit promises and emergencies can create follow-up consequences. Avatar-aligned typing feedback appears after a single send, with relationship-aware but bounded response timing.

## 8. Memory, knowledge, and independent life

Show a source-linked memory, the group thread, and Moments. Explain that a character knows only information attributed to them in the current branch, and their work, travel, friendships, and boundaries continue outside the player’s chat.

## 9. Branch isolation

Save an EchoLink archive node, send an additional message, and rewind. Confirm that relationship state, messages, promises, knowledge, and the sequential mainline phase rewind together without accumulating across branches.

## 10. Portability and prototype boundary

Export the schema-v7 JSON archive. It contains onboarding, all five journeys, mainline phase, confirmed anchors, final decision, EchoLink, creator drafts, and isolated branch nodes. Schema-v6 imports preserve existing relationships and start the new formal investigation at Wen Sheng.

“Real calls, recorded voice, background notifications, infinite procedural worlds, and a production AI backend are outside this offline prototype.”
