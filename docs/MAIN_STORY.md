# EchoLoom main story: After the Seventeenth Second

## Player-readable premise

The player is a trainee observer at the Fate Observation Station. On their first day, archive worker Xu Cheng lends them a temporary access card. A seventeen-second outage interrupts the player’s identity registration. When the lights return, Xu Cheng is gone, her name has vanished from the card, the station has no record of her, and everyone else says they never met her.

Thirty-seven days later, a damaged EchoLink message arrives: “Do you still remember me?” Five anomaly signals appear at the same time.

The mainline goal is deliberately concrete: **find enough independent evidence to determine whether Xu Cheng truly existed.** The player is not introduced as a chosen saviour, and Xu Cheng is never reduced to a reward or a file owned by the player.

## Story legibility rule

Every chapter follows the same visible structure:

1. one question the player can understand;
2. one named ally and one investigation action;
3. one answer retained as an existence anchor;
4. one new question that points directly to the next ally.

Relationship choices alter tone, trust, safety, reciprocity, or compatibility. They never remove the evidence or block the next chapter. Free text remains available beside three intent-only suggestions.

## Relationship prologue

Before the fixed investigation begins, the player may answer any of the five simultaneous first-response signals. That choice determines the first ordinary collaboration, first contact permission, and first relationship impression. It does not assign a romantic route and does not reorder the formal evidence chain.

Wen Sheng’s damaged-dream verification includes retryable audio fragments. A wrong fragment explains the mismatch and allows another attempt; it does not close the mainline.

## Finale: Let Her Return

The primary lens asks whether existence is decided by a system record or by memory, relationships, and choice together. The player receives three non-romantic outcomes:

- **Restore Xu Cheng** writes the five anchors into the current world. She returns, while the protocol begins tracking the group.
- **Preserve her echo** keeps the current world stable, preserves her consent-based EchoLink and memory backup, and begins a search for a safe world.
- **Publish the Unique Line Protocol** makes the evidence available to everyone affected so no single system decides the future. This collaborative option requires basic trust with all five allies.

After any outcome, Xu Cheng becomes an available EchoLink contact because she consents to the connection. Her first reply states that she does not want to be treated only as a restored archive.

## Second arc: The First Free Thread

Resolving Xu Cheng's investigation opens the sealed Weaver Room instead of immediately exposing an unrestricted editor. Xu Cheng reveals **EchoLoom · the Echo Weaver**, a system once used to stabilise people and worlds that the Primary Lens could not classify. The room contains Echo 000, a faceless personality outline scheduled for automatic clearance.

The player reaches Observer Lv.6 and completes five story-led stability threads:

| Thread | Guide | What the player learns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Xu Cheng | A name begins an identity but never gives ownership. |
| Desire | Wen Sheng | A person needs a want that exists outside the player's requests. |
| Memory | Qi An | Personality should grow from experience, while the person retains the right to reinterpret it. |
| Boundary | Zhou Tang | Obedience is not stability; refusal, privacy, and disagreement are coherent behaviour. |
| World | Li Chuan | A person needs a place with rules, conflict, a future, and a route out. |

Every thread retains free text beside intent-only suggestions. Shen Yao then verifies that the five threads create real tension rather than a compliant command profile. This raises the player to Lv.7 and starts the free first-creation tutorial.

The tutorial creates **Nan Xing**, a night-station archivist who fears being forgotten, and **Tide City**, which exists from 00:17 to 06:17 and loses unsaved memories at dawn. The player can change every tutorial field. Confirming the first NPC and world is free, establishes the first personal story opening, raises the player to Lv.8, and grants the Weaver identity.

## Weaver Workshop rules

- The workshop unlocks only at Lv.8 after completing *The First Free Thread*.
- Each local calendar month provides three confirmed NPC creations and one confirmed world creation.
- Only **Confirm creation** consumes capacity. Drafting, previewing, and editing existing content do not.
- The free tutorial NPC and world never consume capacity.
- Monthly calibration resets only the count of new creations. Existing characters and worlds never reset or disappear.
- Mainline progression always uses the free tutorial world and can never be blocked by monthly capacity.
- Every custom NPC records a source thread, desire, tension, memory, initial relationship, and boundary; no setting can guarantee affection or compliance.

## Agency and continuity rules

- Qi An, Shen Yao, Li Chuan, Zhou Tang, and Wen Sheng remain independent people with work, flaws, boundaries, and relationships outside the player.
- Friendship, disagreement, cautious cooperation, and chosen distance are complete relationship outcomes.
- The first-response route remains branch-isolated and recoverable; the formal mainline then proceeds in the same evidence order for clarity.
- Every anchor is source-linked, confidence-labelled, and attributed only to the people who actually observed it.
- Xu Cheng’s identity is restored or preserved with her agency intact; the player is deciding how to respond to the evidence and risk, not deciding what she must feel.

## Current offline-demo boundary

The offline prototype implements the full entry and investigation arc, five existence anchors, three Xu Cheng decisions, the sealed Weaver Room, Echo 000's five stability threads, editable free first NPC/world creation, Lv.8 workshop access, transparent monthly creation capacity, and persistent editable local creations. Existing relationship, memory, EchoLink, and branch-rewind surfaces remain available.
