# Product constraints reference

- Story first, relationship second.
- Stable character core with learnable and growable peripheral details.
- Character has a life outside the user.
- Free input is primary.
- Suggested replies express intent only.
- Short natural replies by default.
- Persistent, visible, editable, exportable memory.
- Main timeline and non-main experiences must not contaminate each other accidentally.
- No streaks, coins, quotas, guilt, coercive jealousy, or punishment for absence.
- No claims that the AI is conscious.
- No direct copying of a protected game UI or character.
